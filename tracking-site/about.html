How it works

From checkout to doorstep, fully automated

Our platform connects directly to your store, generates tracking for every order, and keeps your customers informed at every stage — without any manual work.

01

Order placed

A customer completes a purchase on your connected store

02

Shipment created

Our system generates a unique tracking number and shipment record

03

Events generated

Tracking events are created automatically as the parcel moves through the network

04

Delivered

Customer receives their parcel with full tracking history available

Technology

Built for reliability at scale

Every component of our infrastructure is designed for speed, accuracy, and uptime — because a missed tracking update means a worried customer.

Automated event pipeline

Tracking events are generated and sequenced automatically based on delivery timelines, carrier data, and geographic routing.

Multi-region infrastructure

Deployed across EU and US data centres for low latency regardless of where your customers or stores are located.

Store integration

Native connectors for WooCommerce and Shopify. Orders are synced, fulfilled, and tracked without leaving the platform.

Secure by default

All data encrypted in transit and at rest. JWT-based authentication, role-based access control, and full audit logging.

Global reach

Delivering across borders

Our logistics network spans the European Union, the United Kingdom, and selected international markets — with new regions added regularly.

  • France, Germany, Netherlands, Belgium, Denmark
  • Spain, Portugal, Italy, Austria, Switzerland
  • Poland, Czech Republic, Sweden, Finland, Norway
  • United Kingdom (England, Scotland, Wales)
  • Selected international routes on request
